What it does

Antenna TV only
Plays free over-the-air channels from a SiliconDust HDHomeRun (or black Tablo). Nothing else.
Automatic discovery
Finds tuners on your network. Or type the IP address. Channels and guide load from the tuner.
Full TV guide
Live preview above the guide. Remote-friendly on Android TV and Fire TV. Works on tablets too.
Installs beside WygTV
App ID com.wygtv.ota — keep both apps if you want IPTV and antenna TV.

🔥 Install on Fire TV with Downloader

Fire TV can't install apps from the web on its own, so use the free Downloader app by AFTVnews.

  1. Install Downloader. On the Fire TV home screen choose Find → Search, type Downloader, and install the orange app.
  2. Allow it to install apps. Go to Settings → My Fire TV → Developer options → Install unknown apps and turn Downloader on.
    No Developer options? Open Settings → My Fire TV → About and press OK on your device name 7 times.
  3. Enter the address. Open Downloader and in the URL box type:
    https://github.com/wyglaf01/wygota/releases/latest/download/wygota.apk
    then press Go.
  4. Install. When the download finishes, choose Install, then Done.
  5. Open WygOTA from Your Apps & Games and follow the setup.
